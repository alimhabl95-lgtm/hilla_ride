Full description:
Hello Tuk-Tuk is a local multi-service app for tuk-tuk rides and business delivery in Al-Hashimiya District, Babil Governorate, Iraq.

For passengers:
• Book tuk-tuk rides with live map tracking and in-app chat
• Browse Stores for restaurants, shops, and more — all loaded live from the server
• Order products with real-time prices, offers, and delivery
• See the fare before you book — pay in cash
• Arabic and English throughout

For drivers:
• Go online in your work area and receive nearby ride requests
• Accept ready delivery orders from partner businesses
• Navigate to pickup and destination on the map
• Track wallet, earnings, and platform commission

Hello Tuk-Tuk is built for our community — simple, fast, and always up to date without forcing app updates for new businesses.
