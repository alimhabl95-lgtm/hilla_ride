Hello Tuk-Tuk — iPhone 6.5-inch App Store screenshots
========================================================

Required size: 1284 x 2778 pixels (also accepted: 1242 x 2688)

WHY SIZE MATTERS
----------------
Your phone captures were 576 x 1024 (9:16 ratio).
iPhone 6.5-inch App Store requires 1284 x 2778 (taller 9:19.5 ratio).
Uploading the raw phone capture fails Apple's size check.

These files are converted from your real app screenshots:
- Scaled proportionally (no stretching)
- Center-cropped to exactly 1284 x 2778

UPLOAD THESE (App Store Connect → 6.5" Display)

Regenerate after new captures
-----------------------------
1. Take screenshots on your phone (any size).
2. Save PNG files into the project.
3. Update paths in scripts/resize_ios_screenshots.py
4. Run:  python scripts/resize_ios_screenshots.py

Best quality tip
----------------
For perfect fit with no crop, capture on iPhone 14 Plus / 15 Plus / Max
(simulator or device) at native resolution, then upload directly.
